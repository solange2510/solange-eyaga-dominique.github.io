A LITTLE ABOUT ME

Security with
purpose.

I’m Solange Dominique EYAGA NDEME, a computer engineer specializing in cybersecurity, with a particular interest in governance, risk management and compliance (GRC).

My background has allowed me to build a strong technical foundation in information systems, networking, software development, Cloud and security. Through my experiences at Camtel, ENEO, Tekno-Family and Rotation Trans Logistics, I gradually focused my career on cybersecurity, with a particular interest in governance and risk management.

I am currently pursuing a Master’s degree in Cybersecurity & Management at EFREI Paris, while developing my expertise in ISO 27001, EBIOS Risk Manager, GDPR, NIS2, DORA, auditing and risk management. My hands-on experience with SIEM, particularly FortiSIEM, also gives me a practical understanding of security operations.

I enjoy connecting technical challenges, risks and business needs to turn cybersecurity issues into concrete actions: analyzing, structuring, securing and supporting organizations in their security journey.
